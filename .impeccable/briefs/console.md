## Direction contract

THESIS: Every unknown wallet has a destination. NOIR is a wayfinding system that routes a wallet to the VASP it must be written to — signage, not a dashboard. It refuses the category default of a dark intelligence console with glowing graphs and cards.

OWN-WORLD: White ground #FFFFFF, sign-black ink #111111, signal yellow #FFCC00 as solid destination fields (the only colour at scale), route blue #0A4FD6 for links/actions only, rule grey #D4D4D0. Heavy grotesque (Archivo 800/900 condensed-width headings, Archivo 400/500 body), JetBrains Mono for addresses and figures. Square corners, 2px black rules, arrow glyphs as structure, pictogram-like chain badges. No shadows, no gradients.

STORY: The officer pastes a wallet, sees in one glance which VASP it routes to (outbound: deposit address; inbound: funding exchange), how sure the route is, whether the VASP is FIU-IND registered and how to reach it, and issues the request.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left black sidebar (NOIR wordmark in yellow on black, route list). Main: a destination sign — big yellow block with the VASP name at 64px+, an arrow from the wallet, confidence and evidence tier as sign sub-lines; primary action "Draft request" as a black block button right of the sign.

FORM: Wayfinding signage (International Typographic / transit), position 6 of 7 on the grounded list; seed key efba3248. Raises: all colour spent in one place (civic bureau); rank carried by type size alone (festival lineup); dense tabular mono columns for addresses (datamatics).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
