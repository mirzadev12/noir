## Pinned by the user (30 Sep, later): a serious blockchain-tracking console — extends the pins below

"It is not giving the vibe of a serious crypto and blockchain tracking website; make it animations, graphics and an MVP-level website." Built as, in NOIR's own grammar: the hero now shows the work itself, one real recorded wallet traced both ways as a fund-flow graph (the exchange that funded it, the wallet, the hop, the exchange account it reached, and an OFAC-listed address its money reached as a red branch) in the glowing panel, with a ticker of the transfers the chain read held beneath it and a faint dot grid on the hall's floor. The graph draws once from the wallet outward and light keeps travelling its edges. The same graph heads every wallet's page, drawn from that wallet's own record. The departures board moves to its own section under the hero; the two lit direction panels and the separate recorded route are replaced by the graph, which says both directions at once. Nothing in a graph is typed in or illustrative: a node, an edge or a figure the record does not hold is not drawn, and funders that were not read are said to be unread. The motion has one focal sequence (the trace drawing); every loop rests when it is out of sight and under reduced motion the graph stands whole and still. The palette, the type, the sidebar, the white letter and every truth rule stand.

## Direction contract (as the pins stand)

THESIS: Every unknown wallet has a destination. NOIR is a departures hall at night: a routing console that sends each wallet to the VASP it must be written to. Its grammar is wayfinding (one destination sign per screen, arrows, a drawn route line, a departures board) and the trace itself (each wallet's fund-flow graph, drawn from its own record), not a dashboard of charts and cards.

OWN-WORLD: Night ground #07080B, raised panels one tone lighter (#0E1015, #151821), white type (#F4F5F7) in IBM Plex Sans, JetBrains Mono for anything a machine produced. One electric-blue accent (#3B82F6, deep ends #2563EB and #1E3A8A) that marks direction, light blue (#60A5FA) for links and actions only, and a status trio (green, amber, red) that only ever speaks for status and always also says its meaning in words. The light has named places and no others: the landing's halls, its footer's horizon, the glow on the landing's trace panel and board and on the destination sign. Hairline rules, controls rounded at 0.75rem, the landing's rooms at 1.75rem. The request letter is always a white paper sheet.

STORY: The officer pastes a wallet, sees in one glance which VASP it routes to (outbound: the deposit account; inbound: the funding exchange), how much evidence was seen, whether the VASP is FIU-IND listed and how to reach it, and drafts the one request for every wallet from every case that leads there; then records what the VASP did and watches the clock on it.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The sidebar on the left (the two-tone wordmark, five routes). On the landing: the lit hall, the claim centred in two lines, the second in the accent gradient, two buttons, and one recorded wallet traced both ways as a fund-flow graph, stepping out of the hall's lower edge. On the desk: the destination sign naming the next VASP to write to, in sign type, with its one white action.

FORM: Wayfinding signage carried into a night console; seed key efba3248. Raises: all light spent in named places (the One Light Rule); rank carried by type size alone; dense tabular mono columns for addresses and figures.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
